functionality/dispatch-and-offers.md · POLICY: dispatch.offerTtlMs

Offers are rows in dispatch_offer with a generation. A driver holds at most one live offer, and the accept transaction re-checks ownership, expiry on the database clock, generation and trip state. Losing a race returns trip_taken and the app shows “taken by another driver”.
