Your first day

  1. Read docs/onboarding/README.md and open your role guide.
  2. Follow workflows/local-setup.md: pnpm dev:up, seed, migrate.
  3. Run pnpm verify once, so you know the gate is green on your machine.
  4. Re-record one video with its harness and watch it.
  5. Pick a first task from the end of your guide.

Questions about a rule? It is in docs/POLICY.md. Questions about a decision? 99-decisions/.
