Key invariants: one active ride per driver, enforced by the driver_active_assignment table across solo and shared trips. Booking and dispatch intent commit together through the transactional outbox. Offers, assignments and pool membership live in Postgres; Redis keeps the driver geo index, BullMQ timers and versioned projections that the reconciler rebuilds on boot. Every business knob is in one schema, docs/POLICY.md.
